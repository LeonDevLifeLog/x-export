Three steps to keep a post

  1. Sign in to X. Open a post detail or complete article, expand the text and wait for it to load.
  2. Click X Export and read the current post. Review your selection and choose a reading style.
  3. Export Markdown or HTML, or choose Save as PDF in Chrome printing.

Only already-loaded, accessible content is read. There is no automatic scrolling, video download, or access-control bypass. Image embedding requests optional access to X’s image CDN. You can decline and still export text with original-image links.
